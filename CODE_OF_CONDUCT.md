# Code of Conduct

At Bryde Ud repositories are professional working environments, open to
contributors, partners and agents. This document sets the expected standard of
behaviour in every organization repository, issue, pull request and discussion.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct, be respectful, and assume competence.
- Critique the work, not the person. Review is about the change, not its author.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith, and say when you are uncertain.
- Respect the authority and boundaries defined in
  [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) — including when you disagree with them.
- Keep confidential and sensitive information out of public spaces. See
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or processes.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately circumventing security, governance or review controls.
- Misrepresenting authorship, authority or approval — including presenting
  agent-generated work as reviewed when it has not been.

## Scope

Applies in all At Bryde Ud repositories and organization spaces, and whenever
someone is representing the organization in public. It applies equally to work
submitted with AI assistance: the person who submits a contribution is
accountable for its content and its conduct.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the At Bryde Ud organization owners. Until
a dedicated conduct contact is published here, use GitHub private vulnerability
reporting on [`ecosystem-governance`](https://github.com/atbrydeud/ecosystem-governance/security)
to open a private channel, or contact an organization owner directly. Do not
raise conduct concerns in public issues.

Reports are handled confidentially. Organization owners decide and apply any
response, which may range from a private request to change behaviour up to
removal of access.
